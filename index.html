VESTIGE

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 Android · com.vestige_explore.app
The short version

Vestige has no concept of an account, has no remote server, and no tracking that leaves your phone. Where you walk is saved on your phone and stays there. The only time it uses the internet is when you download a map.

No one else has a copy of where you've been, including the developer.

01What this covers

This policy is about the Vestige Android app. Vestige is a map that starts completely fogged - walking reveals the ground you've actually covered. It's made by one person, with no company, advertisers, or analytics behind it.

Questions about this policy or your data? Open an issue at github.com/DavidLandup0/vestige-pages/issues.

02What the app records

The app does need one thing to work: your location. It records where you walk and turns that into your walking history. That history is saved on your phone and nowhere else.

Each recorded position is stored with its time, how accurate the phone reported it as, and your speed at that moment. The app keeps these positions, not just the revealed map, so a full timed track of your walks stays on the phone until you erase it (see section 08).

There's no account and no ID assigned to you. There are no analytics, ads, or crash reporters, and the app can't access your contacts, files, calendar, or other apps.

03Location

  • It all happens on your phone. Your walks are recorded, drawn onto the map, and used to work out your distances and percentages, all on the device.
  • It keeps recording with the screen off. With background location allowed, the app keeps working while your phone is in your pocket, so the map is up to date when you come back. The notification you see is just how Android keeps that running.
  • Positions come from Google Play Services. Android provides your location through it, following your phone's location settings.

04What's kept on your phone

Three things, in the app's private storage:

  • your walking history and the map it has revealed,
  • your settings,
  • any maps you've downloaded.

Your history isn't included in automatic Android backups. Deleting the app means the recordings are gone. There's no copy anywhere else, unless you made one with Export (see section 08).

Your history is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. It isn't encrypted beyond what your phone already does to its storage.

05When the app uses the internet

Only while you're downloading a map.

If a future version ever adds a new connection, this page will be updated first.

06Sharing and selling

Vestige doesn't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone such as advertisers or data brokers.

Nothing in the app could do so: your data stays on your phone, and the app never sends it anywhere. The only way it leaves your phone is if you export it yourself.

07Permissions

The app only asks for what it needs to record walks:

  • Location, including background - so it can reveal the map where you've walked, even while the app is closed.
  • Notifications - Android requires the app to show one to keep recording in the background. Without it, Android may pause recording for long stretches once you switch away.
  • Start at boot - so recording picks up again after you restart your phone.

08How long it's kept

The developer has no copy of your data. Your history stays on your phone until you delete it:

  • Erase walking data in Settings deletes your whole walking history. Downloaded maps are kept.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes everything, maps included.

Export walking data in Settings saves your history as a file in a folder you choose, to keep it or move it to another phone. The file contains every recorded position. Where it goes after that is up to you: if you save it to a folder that syncs to a cloud service, it is stored there too.

You can stop background recording any time with Track in background in Settings, or in Android's settings.

09Children

Vestige isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect personal data from anyone, children included.

10Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that matters, this page gets updated and the date above moves with it. The full history of changes is public in the repository that hosts this page.

11Contact
